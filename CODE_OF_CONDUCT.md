Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy toward other contributors.

Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

Enforcement Guidelines

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. They will review and address reports fairly.

Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
